Learn — Crypto Basics
💼

What is a crypto wallet?

Think of it like your purse or wallet — but for digital money. It holds your Bitcoin and USDT. Nobody can access it without your password and your secret recovery phrase.

₿

What is Bitcoin (BTC)?

Bitcoin is digital money that works without a bank. It can be sent to anyone in the world in seconds. The price changes daily like gold or foreign currency. Bhutan already mines Bitcoin using hydropower.

💲

What is USDT?

USDT is a stable digital currency always worth exactly 1 US Dollar. Unlike Bitcoin its price does not go up and down. Good for everyday payments if you want a stable amount.

📋

What is a wallet address?

Your wallet address is like your bank account number — but for crypto. You share it with people who want to send you money. It looks like: bc1qxy2kgd... It is safe to share.

🔑

What is a seed phrase?

Your seed phrase is 12 or 24 words that can recover your wallet if you lose your phone. Write it on paper. Never share it with anyone — not even DrukShift. Anyone with your seed phrase can access all your money.

🔲

How does a QR code payment work?

The shop shows their QR code. You scan it with your phone. The wallet reads the shop address and amount automatically. You confirm and the payment is sent instantly — no cash, no card, no bank needed.

Safety tip for Bhutanese users:
Never send crypto to someone who promises to double your money. Never share your seed phrase. Only send money to people and shops you trust. If something seems too good to be true — it is a scam.

Follow these steps on your mBoB app
1

Open mBoB on your phone

Open the Bank of Bhutan mBoB app and log in with your username and password or fingerprint.

mBoB App → Login
2

Tap "Fund Transfer"

On the mBoB home screen tap Fund Transfer then select Interbank Transfer to send to another bank account.

mBoB → Fund Transfer → Interbank
3

Enter exchange account details

Enter the DrukShift Exchange account number shown below. This is the licensed exchange that converts your Ngultrum to crypto.

DSHIFT-EXCHANGE-BT-001
Bank: DrukShift Licensed Exchange · Bhutan

Enter Nu. 5,000 and your reference

Enter the amount Nu. 5,000 in the transfer amount field. In the Remarks box type your DrukShift Wallet ID so the exchange knows to credit your wallet.

DEKI-BT-2026-SILK

Confirm with your mPIN

mBoB will ask for your 4-digit transaction PIN to confirm. Enter it and tap Confirm Transfer.

mBoB → Enter mPIN → Confirm

Wait 2-5 minutes
